# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Hold Shift over the XP bar to turn it into a level 1 to 60 progress bar, filled in green with Blizzard's own bar art.
- The Shift tooltip shows the percentage to level 60, your level, XP earned and remaining, and rested XP.
- The Shift tooltip estimates the time left to level 60 and your total /played at 60, from your average XP per hour played.
- XP per level starts from the Classic table and is replaced with the values the game reports as characters reach each level.
